## ✨ What Changed

### 1. Overlay
- **Before**: Heavy dark gradient (95% opacity)
- **After**: Cleaner gradient (95% → 70% → 30%)
- **Result**: More of the hotel lobby visible, professional look

### 2. Headline Text
- **Before**: `text-5xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl` (too large)
- **After**: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl` (perfect size)
- **Result**: Better proportions, matches reference

### 3. Subtitle
- **Before**: `text-xl md:text-2xl` (too large)
- **After**: `text-lg md:text-xl` (right size)
- **Result**: Cleaner hierarchy

### 4. Buttons
- **Before**: Large padding `px-8 py-4`
- **After**: Compact `px-6 py-3`
- **Result**: Better proportions, modern look

### 5. Feature Icons
- **Before**: `w-8 h-8` icons, larger spacing
- **After**: `w-6 h-6` icons, compact grid
- **Result**: Tighter, more professional

### 6. Layout
- **Before**: Two-column grid with empty right side
- **After**: Single column, left-aligned
- **Result**: Hotel image visible throughout
